About Us

Bookmybeats is a Gaborone-based DJ booking platform that connects event planners with top-tier DJs. At BookMyBeats, we are passionate about music and dedicated to providing exceptional DJ services for all types of event. We offer a wide range of DJ services to suit your needs and budget. Whether you like some slow jams or fast beats, no matter the genre, we have the perfect DJ for your event! We offer Amapiano, Afro-tech, R&B and soo much more!
